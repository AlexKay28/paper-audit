# Aspect 17 — NeurIPS Compliance

**Dimension:** Does the paper satisfy the NeurIPS Paper Checklist and
submission rules? (Papers without the checklist are desk-rejected.)

**Source basis:** `../rules/01-neurips-paper-checklist.md` (all 16 items);
`../rules/03-ml-reviewer-guidelines.md` §NeurIPS;
`../rules/05-pineau-reproducibility-checklist.md` (adopted by NeurIPS).

## How to answer

Answer each item **Yes** / **No** / **N/A**. **Pass if** states which answer
counts as a pass. See `README.md` for scoring. Answering **No** or **N/A** is
not itself grounds for rejection if justified — NeurIPS guidance says honest
disclosure should not be penalized.

## Items

### NP01. Is the NeurIPS Paper Checklist included (not removed)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Papers without the checklist are desk-rejected.
- **Source:** `01` (intro)

### NP02. Is the checklist placed after the references (and optional supplemental)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Placement is mandated.
- **Source:** `01` (How to Answer)

### NP03. Does the checklist not count toward the page limit?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** The checklist is page-limit-exempt; miscounting risks
  over-limit desk rejection.
- **Source:** `01` (intro)

### NP04. Are checklist answers visible to reviewers/ACs/ethics reviewers?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Answers are part of the review record and published with the
  paper.
- **Source:** `01` (How to Answer)

### NP05. Item 1 — Claims: do abstract/intro claims accurately reflect contributions and scope?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 1.
- **Source:** `01` §1

### NP06. Item 2 — Limitations: are limitations of the work discussed?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 2; a separate Limitations section is
  encouraged.
- **Source:** `01` §2

### NP07. Item 3 — Theory: are all assumptions stated and proofs complete?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 3.
- **Source:** `01` §3

### NP08. Item 4 — Reproducibility: is there a reasonable avenue to verify results?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 4; code release not required but some
  path must exist.
- **Source:** `01` §4

### NP09. Item 5 — Code/Data: are code, data, and instructions included for main results?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 5.
- **Source:** `01` §5

### NP10. Item 6 — Experimental details: are training details (splits, hyperparameters, selection) specified?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 6.
- **Source:** `01` §6

### NP11. Item 7 — Statistical significance: are error bars / significance reported suitably?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 7.
- **Source:** `01` §7

### NP12. Item 8 — Compute: are compute resources (type, memory, time) disclosed?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 8.
- **Source:** `01` §8

### NP13. Item 9 — Ethics: is conformity to the NeurIPS Code of Ethics acknowledged?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 9.
- **Source:** `01` §9

### NP14. Item 10 — Broader impacts: are potential negative societal impacts discussed where appropriate?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 10.
- **Source:** `01` §10

### NP15. Item 11 — Safeguards: for high-risk dual-use models, are safeguards described?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 11.
- **Source:** `01` §11

### NP16. Item 12 — Licenses: are used assets cited with license and version?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 12.
- **Source:** `01` §12

### NP17. Item 13 — New assets: are released assets documented (template, license, consent)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 13.
- **Source:** `01` §13

### NP18. Item 14 — Crowdsourcing/human subjects: are instructions, screenshots, and compensation described?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 14; workers must be paid ≥ minimum wage.
- **Source:** `01` §14

### NP19. Item 15 — IRB: are participant risks and IRB approval described where applicable?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 15.
- **Source:** `01` §15

### NP20. Item 16 — LLM usage: is non-standard LLM use in core methods declared?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS checklist item 16; writing/editing assistance need
  not be declared.
- **Source:** `01` §16

### NP21. Are "no" or "n/a" answers accompanied by a 1–2 sentence justification?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Justifications are expected; unjustified "no"/"n/a" is a
  riskier signal.
- **Source:** `01` (How to Answer)

### NP22. Are checklist answers consistent with the paper content (no contradictions)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Inconsistencies between checklist and paper erode trust.
- **Source:** `01` (How to Answer)

### NP23. Is the paper evaluated as written (not as it could be with revisions)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** NeurIPS reviewers are instructed to evaluate the paper as
  written.
- **Source:** `03` §NeurIPS (Reviewer Instructions)

### NP24. Are honest limitations acknowledged without penalty (per NeurIPS guidance)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Reviewers are instructed not to penalize honest limitation
  disclosure.
- **Source:** `01` §2; `03` §NeurIPS

### NP25. For closed-source models, is some verification path provided (e.g. registered access)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Closed-source models still require a path to verification.
- **Source:** `01` §4
