# Aspect 19 — ICML Compliance

**Dimension:** Does the paper meet ICML's review structure and calibration
expectations — including the required review sections, calibration bands, and
emphasis on reproducibility, rigor, writing, and novelty?

**Source basis:** `../rules/03-ml-reviewer-guidelines.md` §ICML;
`../rules/05-pineau-reproducibility-checklist.md` (ICML uses an extended
version); `../rules/01-neurips-paper-checklist.md` (shared items).

## How to answer

Answer each item **Yes** / **No** / **N/A**. **Pass if** states which answer
counts as a pass. See `README.md` for scoring.

## Items

### IM01. Does the paper enable a Summary (brief description of contributions)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML reviews open with a Summary; the paper should support
  one.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Review Structure)

### IM02. Are Strengths clearly identifiable (3–5 specific positive aspects)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML reviews list Strengths; the paper should surface them.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Review Structure)

### IM03. Are Weaknesses addressable (3–5 specific concerns with proposed fixes)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML reviews list Weaknesses; papers should minimize obvious
  ones.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Review Structure)

### IM04. Are likely reviewer Questions anticipated (clarifications)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML reviews include Questions; preempting them helps.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Review Structure)

### IM05. Is there a stated Limitations assessment (ICML review field)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML reviews include a Limitations field; the paper should
  state them.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Review Structure)

### IM06. Are ethics concerns addressed (ICML review field)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML reviews include an Ethics field; concerns should be
  preempted.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Review Structure)

### IM07. Is the paper calibrated for a typical accepted paper (score 4–5)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML calibration: typical accepted paper = 4–5.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Scoring Guidelines)

### IM08. Is the paper strong enough for top-25% of accepted (score 5–6)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Top-25% calibration band; aspirational.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Scoring Guidelines)

### IM09. Does the paper avoid the borderline zone (score 3–4)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Borderline papers lean reject; avoid the borderline signals.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Scoring Guidelines)

### IM10. Does the paper avoid clear-reject signals (score 1–2)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Clear-reject signals are technical flaws or weak evaluation.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Scoring Guidelines)

### IM11. Is reproducibility sufficient (enough details to verify)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML key evaluation point 1.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Key Evaluation Points)

### IM12. Is experimental rigor present (multiple seeds, proper baselines)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML key evaluation point 2.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Key Evaluation Points)

### IM13. Is writing quality clear, organized, and well-structured?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML key evaluation point 3.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Key Evaluation Points)

### IM14. Is the contribution non-trivial (novelty)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML key evaluation point 4.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Key Evaluation Points)

### IM15. Is the extended Pineau reproducibility checklist satisfiable?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML uses an extended version of Pineau's checklist.
- **Source:** `05` (Relationship to Conference Checklists)

### IM16. Are baselines tuned with the same budget as the proposed method?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML rigor point; unequal budgets bias comparisons.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML; `05` §16

### IM17. Are error bars / variance reported with methodology?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML rigor point.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML; `05` §12

### IM18. Are hyperparameters documented (selection process, search ranges)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML rigor point.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML; `05` §9

### IM19. Are proofs complete (in main or appendix) where theoretical results are claimed?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML expects complete proofs for theory contributions.
- **Source:** `01` §3; `03` §ICML

### IM20. Is the paper well-organized for an ICML-caliber review?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Organization is part of ICML's writing-quality criterion.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Key Evaluation Points)

### IM21. Is the contribution clearly distinguished from prior work?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Novelty is an ICML key evaluation point.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Key Evaluation Points)

### IM22. Does the paper avoid demanding unreasonable additional experiments?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Reviewers are told not to demand unreasonable experiments;
  the paper should pre-empt common asks.
- **Source:** `03` §NeurIPS (What Reviewers Should Avoid — shared across venues)

### IM23. Is the paper self-contained for an ICML reviewer?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Self-containedness is expected; reliance on undisclosed
  external material fails.
- **Source:** `03` §Clarity

### IM24. Is the Limitations section honest (not token)?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** ICML's Limitations review field assesses honesty.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Review Structure)

### IM25. Is the paper free of technical flaws that would trigger a clear reject?
- **Pass if:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Technical flaws or weak evaluation are clear-reject signals.
- **Source:** `03` §ICML (Scoring Guidelines)
