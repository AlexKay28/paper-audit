# ICLR Author Guide

**Source:** <https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2025/AuthorGuide>
**Fetched:** Aug 19, 2026 (ICLR 2025)

## Submission Timeline (ICLR 2025)

- **Abstract submission deadline:** September 27, 2024 (AoE)
- **Full paper submission deadline:** October 1, 2024 (AoE)
- Submit at: <https://openreview.net/group?id=ICLR.cc/2025/Conference>
- Deadlines are **final**; no accommodations for missing either.
- Abstracts must be genuine and informative — they are used in reviewer bidding
  before the full-paper deadline. Placeholder or duplicate abstracts are removed.

> For detailed submission instructions (paper length, style file), see the
> Call for Papers: <https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers>

## OpenReview Profiles

- **All authors are required to have an OpenReview profile.**
- Co-authors without profiles by the abstract deadline do **not** cause desk
  rejection, as long as all authors have profiles before the full-paper
  deadline.
- Authors cannot be added or removed after the abstract deadline; ordering can
  be changed (only until the submission deadline) if contributions change.

## Source Code Submission

- Source code may be uploaded as supplementary material — encouraged but not
  required for reviewers to examine.
- All supplementary code must be **self-contained and zipped into a single
  file**, or downloadable via an anonymous URL.
- Supplementary material is visible to reviewers and the public throughout and
  after the review period. **Ensure all material is anonymized.**

### Three ways to share code anonymously

1. Anonymize code, zip it, submit as supplementary material.
2. Make an anonymous repository and put the link in the paper (code becomes
   public alongside the paper and reviews).
3. After discussion forums open, post a comment to reviewers/ACs with a link to
   an anonymous repository (code visible only to your paper's reviewers/ACs).

## Supplementary (text) Submission

- Submit a single file (paper + supplementary text) where possible.
- Mark supplementary material clearly.
- Appendices may either be at the end of the main PDF (after references) or as a
  separate supplementary file.

## Code of Ethics & Ethics Statement

- All participants must adhere to the [ICLR Code of Ethics](https://iclr.cc/public/CodeOfEthics).
- Authors must read, adhere to, and explicitly acknowledge the Code of Ethics
  during submission.
- Reviewers are encouraged to flag potential violations.
- Authors anticipating ethics concerns should discuss them in a separate section
  (not counted against page limit).
- Authors who encounter violations during review/discussion should raise them in
  a private message to the Area Chair via OpenReview.

### Ethics Statement (optional)

Include a paragraph **at the end of the main text (before references)** to
address potential concerns. Topics include, but are not limited to:

- Studies involving human subjects.
- Practices related to dataset releases.
- Potentially harmful insights, methodologies, or applications.
- Potential conflicts of interest and sponsorship.
- Discrimination / bias / fairness concerns.
- Privacy and security issues.
- Legal compliance.
- Research integrity (IRB, documentation, research ethics).

> The optional ethics statement does **not** count toward the page limit, but
> should be no more than **1 page**.

## Reproducibility Statement

It is important that ICLR work is reproducible. Authors are strongly encouraged
to include a paragraph-long **Reproducibility Statement** *at the end of the main
text (before references)* discussing efforts made to ensure reproducibility.

The statement should **reference** the parts of the main paper, appendix, and
supplemental materials that aid reproducibility — not duplicate those details.
Examples:

- **Novel models/algorithms** → link to anonymous downloadable source code as
  supplementary material.
- **Theoretical results** → clear explanation of assumptions + complete proof in
  the appendix.
- **Datasets** → complete description of data-processing steps in supplementary
  material.

> The optional reproducibility statement does **not** count toward the page
> limit, but should be no more than **1 page**.

## Reviewing Process

Submissions are uploaded to OpenReview, enabling public discussion during
review. Submissions and reviews are anonymous and visible as follows:

- Official reviews are anonymous and publicly visible.
- Any logged-in user may post comments (public, or restricted to reviewers+/ACs+/
  PCs). Login required.

### Key Dates

- **Review Release:** November 13, 2024
- **Discussion Stage:** November 13 – November 26
  - Discussions among Reviewers/Authors/Public.
  - Anyone may post comments (logged in, name shown). Reviewers remain
    anonymous.
  - Authors can update drafts until **Nov 27** (EoD AoE).
- Like 2024, there is **no second stage** of author–reviewer discussion.

### Author Comments

- Authors may post "official comments" once discussion begins, and restrict
  visibility (reviewers, ACs, PCs) as appropriate — e.g., to share supplementary
  material links.

## Camera-Ready Submissions

- Email instructions for uploading camera-ready submissions go out mid-February.
- Page limit identical to submission version (**10 pages**).
- Ethics statements and acknowledgements do **not** count toward page limit.
- Appendix can be part of the main paper (recommended).
- Author orders can be changed; authors cannot be added or removed.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Highlights)

### Before Submission

- **Abstract:** Only "Abstract*" is mandatory at abstract deadline (not PDF).
- **OpenReview accounts:** Required for all co-authors. Verify by clicking
  author names — they should resolve to a profile.
- **Author changes:** None after the abstract deadline (no exceptions). Order
  can be changed only until the submission deadline.
- **Supplementary deadline:** Same as the full-paper deadline.
- **Appendices:** Either at the end of the main PDF (after references) or as a
  separate supplementary file.
- **Anonymous code:** See "Three ways to share code anonymously" above.
- **Demos/videos:** Anonymous links acceptable; host must not track visitors.
- **Submission access issues:** Use the same OpenReview account; click "Revision"
  button. If still stuck, follow profile-merge guidance or contact
  info@openreview.net.
- **Challenge leaderboards:** OK to report ranking and challenge name.
  Reviewers are advised not to search authors via the leaderboard.
- **Placeholder abstracts:** Deleted.
- **arXiv self-citations:** Cite prior/follow-up work in 3rd person. An arXiv
  version does not violate anonymity so long as you do not refer to it
  explicitly.
- **Ethics Statement necessity:** Not required if the submission has no ethics
  issues to the best of the authors' knowledge.
- **Acknowledgements:** Not included in page limit.

### During Discussion Stages

- **Title/abstract revisions:** OK if the revised version stays close to the
  original abstract; a substantively different paper will be rejected.
- **Rebuttal revisions:** Title, abstract, content (including supplementary)
  may be changed. Communicate modifications clearly to reviewers/ACs. Authors
  cannot be added/removed, but order can change.
- **Rebuttal page limit:** Identical to submission (10 pages).
- **Rebuttal uploads:** Multiple revisions allowed until discussion ends;
  reviewers/ACs are not required to examine every revision.
- **Comments:** Word limit per comment, but **no limit on number of comments**.
  Multiple replies per reviewer are allowed; remain civil and considerate of
  reviewer time.
- **Contacting reviewers/ACs/PCs:** Use OpenReview forum; choose appropriate
  readers (Reviewers/ACs of submission). First point of contact = AC. Escalate
  to PCs via email prefixed `[Authors-to-PCs]` only if AC cannot resolve.
- **Registration:** One author of an accepted paper must have a "Conference and
  Workshops" registration (student registration suffices for students). One
  registration covers multiple papers by the same author.

## Other Conference Logistics

- Authors registered for in-person attendance and offered an oral spot present
  orally in person.
- Authors registered for attendance record a 5-minute summary video, hosted on
  iclr.cc for asynchronous viewing.
- SlidesLive recording links are emailed and available on the My Stuff page
  under Presentation History.
- In-person poster sessions exist; **no virtual poster session**.
- Spotlight papers are recognized with a "Spotlight" label on the poster board.
- Invitation letters for visas can be generated after registration
  (<https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2025/VisaTravel>).
